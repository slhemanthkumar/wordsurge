## Full description

Connect letters. Ride the surge.

WordSurge is a polished offline word puzzle where every route challenges you to connect letters, discover words, and complete a crossword before time runs out.

START EASY, MASTER COMPLEX ROUTES
• Basic and Standard routes: a quick 30-second sprint
• Complex and Expert routes: a full 60 seconds for bigger boards
• Difficulty changes naturally so every session stays fresh

EARN REWARDS AS YOU PROGRESS
• Build Surge Combos with consecutive correct words
• Unlock a Surge Bonus every 5 levels
• Open a Treasure Chest every 10 levels
• Earn coins, XP, stars, achievements, and themes
• Receive extra rewards for complex and perfect completions

PLAY EVERY DAY
• Daily rewards based on your device calendar
• Date-based Daily Challenges
• Weekly and monthly progress routes
• Login streaks, Lucky Spin, and Mystery Chest rewards

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Works offline
• Smooth one-hand letter tracing
• Smart hints, letter hints, word hints, and shuffle
• Music, sound, vibration, dark mode, larger text, and reduced motion
• Progress is saved automatically on your device
• Designed for phones and tablets

BEAUTIFUL WORLDS
Travel through original environments including Surge Bay, Whispering Forest, Quiet Tundra, Amber Dusk, Misty Peaks, Aurora Vale, Velvet Cosmos, Koi Garden, Moonlit Kingdom, and more.

WordSurge is easy to learn, rewarding to improve at, and built for quick daily word sessions.

Download WordSurge and start your next word route.
