# WordSurge Brand Review

Reviewed 8 September 2026 against Google Play, the Apple App Store and general web results.

## Final selection

**WordSurge** — chosen over the working name *LetterRide* because it puts the game's
differentiator (short, fixed timers) directly into the name.

- "Word" states the category instantly for store search.
- "Surge" conveys speed, energy and the 30–60 second time pressure; it also pairs with the
  ocean-wave art direction (Surge Bay) and the wave-mark logo.
- One word, two syllables, easy to spell and pronounce in India, the US, the UK and beyond.
- Store title: **WordSurge: Timed Word Puzzle** (28/30 characters)
- Tagline: **Connect letters. Ride the surge.**
- Package name: `ai.arena.wordsurge` (permanent after first upload)

## Vocabulary used in the game

- **Surge combo** — consecutive correct words (`Surge ×2`, `Surge ×3` …)
- **Surge Bonus** — every 5th level, +40 coins
- **Treasure Chest** — every 10th level, +120 coins
- **Surge Streak** — achievement for a 5-word combo
- Default player name **Surge Rider**, avatar **W**
- Routes: Basic 30 s · Standard 30 s · Complex 60 s · Expert 60 s
