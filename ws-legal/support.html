WordSurge Support

WordSurge is an offline timed word puzzle for Android 8.0 and newer. Package: ai.arena.wordsurge

Quick fixes

  • Blank or frozen screen: update Android System WebView from Google Play and reopen the game.
  • Wrong date on calendar widgets: check the device date, time and time zone (Settings → System → Date & time, "Set automatically").
  • No sound: check Music and Sound effects in the game's Settings and the device media volume.
  • Keep your progress: use Settings → Export local save before resetting or changing phones.

Reporting a problem

Include the app version (shown on the Google Play listing), Android version, device model, the level number and the steps to reproduce. Never send passwords or payment details.
