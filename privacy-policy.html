WordSurge Privacy Policy

Effective 7 September 2026 · Applies to WordSurge for Android (package ai.arena.wordsurge) and the web version

WordSurge is an offline word puzzle. It has no account, requests no Android permissions, contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or purchase software, and does not send any information to the developer or to anyone else.

Information stored on your device

Your level progress, coins, hints, settings, achievements, timers, login dates and statistics are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

Date, time and language

WordSurge reads your device's current date, time, time zone and language to show calendar widgets and run daily, weekly and monthly challenges. These values are used on the device only.

Backups

Android may include the app's private data in a device backup or device-to-device transfer according to your Android and Google account backup settings. The developer has no access to those backups.

Links

Buttons in Settings open this policy, the terms, the support page and the Google Play listing in your browser. Those pages are subject to their own hosts' policies.

Children

WordSurge is suitable for a general audience. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Reset progress inside the game, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Any backups are controlled by your own Google account settings.

Changes

If a future version adds any online feature, this policy and the Google Play Data safety section will be updated before that version is released.
